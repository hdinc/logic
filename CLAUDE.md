After every development update, update the `DEVELOPMENT_LOG.md` file with a clear and concise summary of:

- **Changes made:** Modifications, improvements, and bug fixes.
- **Features implemented:** New functionality and completed features.
- **Recommended features:** Suggestions for future enhancements.
- **Outstanding issues:** Known bugs, limitations, and unfinished tasks.
- **Next steps:** Planned improvements and recommended actions.

Maintain the log in chronological order, append new entries without removing previous records, and include a timestamp for each entry.
