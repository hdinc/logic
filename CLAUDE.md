After every development update, update the `DEVELOPMENT_LOG.md` file with a clear and concise summary of:

- **Changes made:** Modifications, improvements, and bug fixes.
- **Features implemented:** New functionality and completed features.
- **How it works:** How the features are implemented (design, key files and functions), and the decisions taken with their reasons, including alternatives that were rejected.
- **Testing:** What was tested and how (unit tests, GUI checks), and the results.
- **Recommended features:** Suggestions for future enhancements.
- **Outstanding issues:** Known bugs, limitations, and unfinished tasks.
- **Next steps:** Planned improvements and recommended actions.

The entry should keep everything the summary given to the user at the end of the work says, so nothing explained there is lost. Update the log before committing.

Maintain the log in chronological order, append new entries without removing previous records, and include a timestamp for each entry.

Don't list committing, pushing or bumping the Hd submodule as next steps or outstanding issues; those are done on request, not planned work.
